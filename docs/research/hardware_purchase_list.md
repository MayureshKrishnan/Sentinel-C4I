## 💡 Notes Before Ordering
* **Jumper Wires & Breadboards**: Assuming standard DuPont jumper wires (Male-to-Female / Female-to-Female) are already available in your college lab. If not, add 1 pack of jumper wires on Robu.in (~₹40).
* **CH340 Driver**: Before plugging the NodeMCU into your laptop via USB, install the free CH340 driver so your laptop recognizes the board on COM port.
